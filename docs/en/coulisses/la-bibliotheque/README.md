# 📚 The Contracts Library

**His job**: file in our library the French **public contracts** — the contracts
awarded by administrations, local authorities and public companies.

Each contract is archived with the important information: who buys, who
supplies, for what amount, for what mission.

**Today: more than one million contracts, from 2015 to now.**

## What the library contains

| Information | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who buys | The local authority or administration |
| Who supplies | The company that won the contract |
| Amount | The contract price |
| Mission | What was purchased (classified by code) |
| Date | When the contract was awarded |

## His promises

| Promise | Detail |
|---|---|
| Complete | Since 2015, updated every day |
