# 🌅 The Morning Postman

**His job**: every morning, very early, collect all the official announcements
of French companies published the day before, and bring them home — without
losing a single one.

He is the **first step** of our story: without him, nothing could ever reach
your screen.

## What he does every day

1. He goes to collect the official announcements (creations, modifications,
   deregistrations, insolvency proceedings, sales, annual accounts...).
2. He brings them back and carefully files them in the library.
3. He warns the translator: "there is work to do!"

## His promises

| Promise | Detail |
|---|---|
| Every morning | Yesterday's announcements are available before 9 am |
| Nothing is lost | Running twice never creates a duplicate |
| Full history | We go back to 2008, not just recent days |
