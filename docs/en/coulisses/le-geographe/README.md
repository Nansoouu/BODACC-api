# 🗺️ The Geographer

**His job**: verify that every company really exists on the world map
(Google Maps) and note what we see there: the address, the category, the
rating, the number of reviews, the website, the phone number.

Thanks to him, a company record also shows its **real presence** in the real
world — not just its official paperwork.

## What he notes

| Information | Detail |
|---|---|
| Address on the map | Where the company really is |
| Category | Restaurants, shops, services... |
| Rating and reviews | What customers say publicly |
| Website / phone | Verified on the public record |

## His promises

| Promise | Detail |
|---|---|
| Public only | Nothing but what everyone can see |
| By campaign | He explores lists of companies in groups |
| Respectful | He moves slowly so as not to disturb anyone |
