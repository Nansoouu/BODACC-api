# 🧩 The People Puzzle

**His job**: find every person who appears in the announcements — directors,
liquidators, statutory auditors, buyers, sellers — and link them to their
companies.

Thanks to him, we can answer simple questions:
- "Which companies does this person run?"
- "Who is behind this company?"
- "Since when?"

## What we know today

| Figure | Detail |
|---|---|
| Person records | 2 684 424 |
| Natural persons | The vast majority (first + last name) |
| Companies as directors | ~1 million (a company can direct another) |
| Directors linked to their record | More than 186 000 in profiles |

## How he works

1. He reads every announcement and spots the people mentioned.
2. He checks whether the person already exists (same name, same first name).
3. He links them to the company and the role (president, manager...).
4. He completes the record with official qualifications.
