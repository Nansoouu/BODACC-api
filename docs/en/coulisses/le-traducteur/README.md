# 📖 The Translator

**His job**: read every official announcement and turn it into a clear,
understandable record: the company name, its address, its activity, its
directors, the amounts, the dates...

An official announcement is an administrative text, often long and complicated.
The translator turns it into a record that anyone can read — a 7-year-old child
or a banker.

## What he does

1. He reads every announcement from start to finish.
2. He extracts the important information: who, what, where, when, how much.
3. He files the result in the relevant company's record.
