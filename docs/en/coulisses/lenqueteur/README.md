# 🔍 The Investigator

**Her job**: complete each company's record with its **public** information —
its website, its phone number, its social networks.

She only looks for what the company shows itself to the general public.
Nothing private, nothing secret, nothing behind a password.

## What she adds to the record

| Information | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Website | What the company displays publicly |
| Phone number | Public number |
| Social networks | Public links (LinkedIn, Instagram...) |
| Presence on the map | Verified by the geographer |
| Directors' qualifications | Official public registers ("President of SAS", "Manager"... ) |

## Her promises

| Promise | Detail |
|---|---|
| Public only | Never private or protected data |
| Progressive | Records get richer over time |
| Verifiable | Every piece of data shows its source |
